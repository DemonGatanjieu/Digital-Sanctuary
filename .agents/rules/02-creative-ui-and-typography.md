# 02. Creative UI Aesthetics & Typography Guidelines

> 🔒 Dedicated Rule for Antigravity / Gemini Workspace Assistant in Digital Sanctuary (纯净领域).

---

## 1. Zero Generic Templates
- **No Generic Bootstrap/Tailwind Styles**: Avoid plain, generic engineer-like UI. Always prioritize distinctive, immersive, and premium visual elements.
- **Rich Aesthetics**:
  - Fine-art glassmorphism and subtle backdrop blurs (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`).
  - Curated HSL ambient palettes and thematic tokens.
  - Floating acrylic control islands and context-aware HUDs.
  - Tactile physical cards, subtle shadow elevations, and fine-art mats.

---

## 2. Typography & Font Protocol Integrity
- **Custom Font Protocol**: Strictly use `sanctuary-font://` protocol for loading user-installed TTF/OTF/WOFF2 typography.
- **Display Hierarchy**: Maintain crisp, high-legibility typographic scale without layout shifts.
- **CJK Font Metrics**: Preserve line-height and font-feature-settings for East Asian and multilingual glyph consistency.
